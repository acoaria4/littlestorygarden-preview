It began with
one little girl.

We made a book for our daughter, Inara. A little alphabet adventure with her face, her Amma, her Appa, and all the wonder of her world.

That first book became the seed of Little Story Garden. Now we’re creating a way for more families to find themselves between the pages.

Made with love, for the people they love.

A few things
you might be wondering.

What makes the book personal?

Your child and parents become illustrated characters, with your child’s name, your family’s parent labels, and a dedication. The alphabet adventure grows around those familiar faces.

Can I check the illustrations first?

Yes. The planned journey starts with individual family characters for you to review and correct. Book pages follow only after the characters are approved.

Is this a printed book?

Yes, Little Story Garden is being created for printed alphabet books for families in India. Paper, binding, pricing, and delivery details will be shared once the print partner and physical samples are finalised.

Can I order a book today?

We’re still preparing for our first families. You can explore the book and try the creation preview, but illustration generation, checkout, and ordering are not available yet.

What happens to photos in this preview?

Photos you choose in the creation preview stay in this browser tab. They are not uploaded or saved, and disappear when you refresh or close it. Private photo storage will be connected before real book creation opens.

Which languages will be available?

We’re starting with English, following Inara’s original alphabet book. Additional languages are a future possibility.
